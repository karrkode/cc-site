              Roundup pages

              Public pages at chiculturecrew.com/chicago/ that list events by when, price, kind and side of town. Each opens with a short intro. Leave one empty to use the default.

              Found events

              Events the checker found on venues' own pages, and changes to events already listed. Approve to add one to the catalog; nothing goes live by itself.

              Skip rules

              Found events whose title contains one of these phrases are rejected quietly. Made with “Reject and skip similar”.

              Event sources

              Venues and companies whose own pages are checked now and then for new events. What's found waits for your review; nothing goes live by itself. A page is only read again when it has changed.

              For reading pages with Claude. At 80%, event pages wait until next month; at 100%, only Check now runs.

              Photo approvals

              Photos members added to "I've been". Approve the ones that show they were there; reject the rest, with a reason they'll see if you like. Amazon Rekognition has already turned away anything explicit; what it wasn't sure about is blurred until you choose to look.

              Members

              Offer a member the approver permission: they can then approve "I've been" photos. They see the offer when they sign in, and it applies once they accept. Admins can always approve.

              Issues and feature requests

              Problems members reported with the site (ISS-), and features they asked for (FR-). Set where each one stands; a note you add shows beside it on the member's account page.
